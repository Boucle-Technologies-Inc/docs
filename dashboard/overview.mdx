Everything you do to run your loyalty program happens at [app.ruffus.app](https://app.ruffus.app) — your Ruffus dashboard. This page walks through the navigation and what you'll find on the overview screen itself.

<Note>
  Dashboard numbers refresh about once a minute, not instantly. If a stamp was just approved at the counter, give it a moment to show up in your charts.
</Note>

## The navigation

The sidebar is six flat entries, no section headings: **Home**, **Customers**, **My cards**, **Campaigns**, **In store**, and **Settings**. Several of those open with a tab strip across the top rather than a further level of sidebar. Support and Billing open from your avatar in the top-right, not from the sidebar.

## What's on the overview page

**Home**'s Overview tab is your home base. At a glance it shows:

- **Stamps today** — how many stamps were approved so far today.
- **Member stats** — your total member count and recent growth.
- **In wallets** — how many members have the card saved to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, split by platform, as a percentage of your total members — plus how many have since removed it.
- **Activity feed** — a running stream of recent stamp requests and redemptions.
- **Close to reward** — customers who are near completing their card, so you know who's about to come back for a redemption. Click through to see them as a filtered segment on [Customers](/dashboard/customers).

<Note>
  Apple Wallet removals are detected precisely, since the device tells Ruffus when a pass is unregistered. Google Wallet has no equivalent removal signal, so the "removed" count only reflects Apple.
</Note>

Scroll further and the detailed view adds:

- A **7-day chart** of stamp activity.
- A **period chart** you can switch between 7, 30, and 90-day windows.
- A **busy times heatmap** — stamp activity by day of week and daypart (morning, lunch, afternoon, evening, night) over the last 90 days, with a "Peak: `day` `time`" chip calling out your busiest slot (for example, "Peak: Saturday 2 PM"). Times are shown in America/Toronto time.
- **Program health** — a summary of how your loyalty program is trending.
- **At-risk members** — customers whose visit pattern suggests they're drifting away, which is also what feeds the automatic win-back emails (see [Customers](/dashboard/customers)). Click through to see them as a filtered segment.

## Owner vs. manager access

Ruffus has two dashboard roles:

- **Owner** — full access to every destination listed above.
- **Manager** — limited to **In store**, and only its Poster & QR, Stamp codes, NFC cards, and Devices tabs. Managers run the floor; they don't see billing, customers, campaigns, or program-wide settings — and not the Staff, Staff playbook, or Practice tabs within In store either.

Staff working the counter itself don't get dashboard accounts at all — they use a 6-digit PIN, set up by the owner from **In store → Staff**. See [Customers](/dashboard/customers) for more on staff access.
