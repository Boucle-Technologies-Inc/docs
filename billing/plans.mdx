All Ruffus plans include the full loyalty toolkit — campaigns, Apple/Google Wallet passes, analytics, automatic win-back emails, and your QR poster. What changes between plans is how many locations you get, plus API access and support level. Everything else is flat on every plan: 3 counter stations per location, 2 [NFC station cards](/loyalty-card/nfc-cards) per location, and up to 3 loyalty cards.

## Add-ons

Every plan can add counter stations, and Growth and Network can add locations, as paid add-ons billed as extra line items on the same subscription (same interval as your plan — monthly add-ons on a monthly plan, annual on annual):

- **Extra counter station** — \$5 CAD/mo, same rate on every plan.
- **Extra location** — \$25 CAD/mo on Growth (up to 2 extra, for 5 locations total — beyond that, Network is the better deal), \$19 CAD/mo on Network (uncapped). Not available on Basic — a second location is the Growth upgrade, not an add-on.

Add-on quantities carry over automatically when you change plans (location add-ons are repriced to the new tier's rate); if a downgrade's cap is lower than what you have (for example, moving from Network to Growth), you'll choose which extra locations stay active, same as [Changing plans](#changing-plans) below.

## Annual billing

Paying annually gets you **2 months free** compared to paying monthly:

- Basic: \$499.90 CAD/yr (equivalent to \$41.66/mo)
- Growth: \$999.90 CAD/yr (equivalent to \$83.33/mo)
- Network: \$2,499.90 CAD/yr (equivalent to \$208.33/mo)

Annual plans are billed upfront for the full year. Switching from monthly to annual billing takes effect immediately, with prorated billing for the switch. Switching from annual back to monthly is treated as a downgrade — see [Changing plans](#changing-plans) below — so it's scheduled for the end of your current billing period instead.

## Free trial

Every new account gets a **14-day free trial** with full feature access — no card to start.

The trial clock starts at your **first real stamp**, not at sign-up. Setting up your card, branding your poster and testing the dashboard cost you no trial days; the 14 days begin the first time a customer actually earns a stamp, from any source (QR, NFC tap, staff grant or a connected store). Imported stamps and Practice Mode rehearsals never start it.

A payment method is required before your free NFC tap cards ship. That card is held as a deposit for the card fees described in [NFC Cards](/loyalty-card/nfc-cards) — it is not charged for a subscription.

<Warning>
  **We never auto-charge at the end of a trial.** If your trial ends without a plan selected, your subscription pauses instead: stamping, joining and redemption stop until you pick a plan and add a payment method. Nothing is deleted when this happens. An account that never records a stamp is closed after 90 days, with a warning email first.
</Warning>

## Changing plans

**Upgrades** — moving to a higher plan, or switching monthly → annual — take effect **immediately**: you get the new features right away, and the difference is prorated so you only pay for the remainder of the current period at the new rate.

**Downgrades** — moving to a lower plan, or switching annual → monthly — are **scheduled for the end of your current billing period**. You keep everything you've already paid for until then, and the new plan and price apply from your next renewal. Changed your mind? You can cancel a scheduled downgrade from **Billing** any time before it takes effect.

If you downgrade below your current location count (for example, Network's 10 locations down to Growth's 3), you'll choose which locations stay active when you schedule the downgrade. The others are deactivated at renewal — not deleted — so their data is preserved if you add locations back later. See [Multi-Location](/dashboard/multi-location) for details.

Loyalty cards are capped at 3 on every plan, so changing plans never affects your cards. (A small number of legacy accounts provisioned above this cap before it was flattened to 3 may still be asked to choose which cards stay active when changing plans.) See [Multiple Cards](/loyalty-card/multiple-cards) for how the cap works.

## Cancelling

You can cancel anytime from **Dashboard → Billing**. Your program stays active through the end of your current paid period — cancelling doesn't cut you off immediately. If you're on an annual plan, cancelling stops the renewal; there's no partial refund for the unused months of the year. You can reactivate anytime before your period ends; after a full cancellation, you'd resubscribe through checkout. Closing your account starts a 30-day window to return your NFC tap cards — see [NFC Cards](/loyalty-card/nfc-cards).

Closing seasonally rather than for good? [Pause instead](#seasonal-pause).

## Seasonal pause

If your business closes for part of the year, pause your subscription instead of cancelling. Open **Dashboard → Billing** and choose **Pause for the season**.

- **Billing stops** for the paused months, and no invoice is raised for that period.
- **Your loyalty program pauses too.** Customers can't join, earn stamps or redeem rewards until you resume — the account is inactive, not running for free.
- **Nothing is lost.** Members, stamps, history, branding and your NFC tap cards all stay exactly as you left them. A pause never opens a card-return window.
- **It ends on its own.** A pause runs for up to **4 consecutive months** and lifts automatically; you can resume earlier in one click from the same page.
- **Once every 12 months.** The allowance is one pause per rolling 12-month period, which is what one off-season means. Past that, cancelling and returning the hardware is the honest option.

A pause neither extends nor restarts a free trial.

We don't refund partial billing periods, except where required by law. If something went wrong on our end, we'll always make it right — reach out to [billing@ruffus.app](mailto:billing@ruffus.app).
