The Ruffus team is a small, remote-first group based in Québec, Canada. Whether you have a quick setup question, a billing concern, or a partnership idea, there's a direct line for each. Replies typically land within one business day, and the team is happy to help in **English**, **French**, or **Spanish**.

<Note>
  **Signed in?** The fastest route is the [support centre](/dashboard/support) in your dashboard — **Dashboard → Support**. Your ticket arrives with your account details attached, gets a reference you can quote, and you can follow it through to resolution instead of digging through a mail thread. The email addresses below stay open for everything else.
</Note>

  The dashboard assistant answers setup and how-to questions from this documentation, and hands the conversation to a ticket — transcript attached — when a human needs to step in. ## Response times

Support emails sent to [hello@ruffus.app](mailto:hello@ruffus.app) are answered within one business day (Monday–Friday, Eastern Time). Messages sent over the weekend or on public holidays are answered on the next business day. Billing and press enquiries follow the same schedule.

## Prefer a contact form?

If you'd rather not open your email client, you can also reach us through the web form at [ruffus.app/en/contact](https://ruffus.app/en/contact). Just fill in your name, email, and message — the form routes your enquiry to the right inbox automatically.
