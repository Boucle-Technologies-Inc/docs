## What data Ruffus collects

Ruffus collects only the information needed to run the service:

- **Account information** — your name, email address, and business details when you sign up as a merchant.
- **Loyalty card activity** — stamps issued, rewards redeemed, and card interactions, tied to a customer's unique card link.
- **Payment information** — billing is handled through a third-party payment provider. Ruffus never stores your full card number or sensitive payment credentials directly.
- **Device and usage data** — browser type, device type, IP address, and how you interact with the dashboard or loyalty card pages. This helps the team diagnose bugs and improve the product.

## How your data is used

Ruffus uses the information it collects to:

- Run and maintain the loyalty platform for merchants and their customers.
- Send transactional notifications (for example, a stamp confirmation or a reward alert).
- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse.
- Comply with legal obligations, including tax and financial record-keeping.

**Ruffus does not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties — ever.**

## Mobile phone numbers and SMS

Ruffus collects your mobile number when you create or access an account with a text-message verification code, and when you choose to receive loyalty texts from a business you've joined. Verification codes and essential account notices are transactional — they go out whether or not you've opted in to anything promotional.

**No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category of information above, and are never sold or shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing.

Promotional texts are optional. The consent checkbox at sign-up is unchecked by default and is never required to create an account or receive a verification code. Withdraw consent any time by replying **STOP** to any message, or by emailing [privacy@ruffus.app](mailto:privacy@ruffus.app). Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply.

## Artificial intelligence

Ruffus uses AI in two places, both in the merchant dashboard: the support assistant, and the article suggestions on the "open a ticket" form. What a merchant types into either is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) along with our public product documentation to generate a reply or suggest articles.

- **Neither feature can see account records, customer lists, or billing data.**
- **Your data is not used to train AI models** — not by Ruffus, and not by our provider, whose agreement with us doesn't permit it.
- Assistant conversations are recorded as they happen. An unescalated conversation is deleted after **90 days**; an escalated one is kept as long as the ticket it created.
- Ruffus does not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. If that changes, this policy is updated before the feature goes live.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data Ruffus holds about you.
- **Correct** any information that is inaccurate or out of date.
- **Delete** your data (subject to legal retention requirements — see below).
- **Export** your data in a portable format.
- **Withdraw consent** for any processing based on consent, at any time.
- **Lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority, such as the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI) in Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC).

To exercise any of these rights, email [privacy@ruffus.app](mailto:privacy@ruffus.app) with your request. The Privacy Officer will respond promptly.

## How long data is kept

Ruffus retains your data for as long as your account is active. After cancellation or account closure, there is a **30-day grace period** — you'll receive a warning email before anything is removed, and you can export your member list (CSV) at any time during that window. After the 30 days, loyalty data is permanently deleted. Certain billing and financial records may be retained longer where required by law.

Security and access logs — including the record of when a member's email address is disclosed to merchant staff — are kept for up to **24 months** so a later access request or complaint can be investigated, then deleted automatically.

## Compliance

Ruffus is designed to comply with:

- **Québec Law 25** (Act to modernize legislative provisions as regards the protection of personal information)
- **PIPEDA** (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, Canada)
- **CASL** (Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation)

If you're located in the European Union, the rights described above also satisfy the **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation).

All data is protected with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. In the event of a data breach, Ruffus will notify the CAI and all affected individuals as required by Law 25.

## International transfers

Ruffus is operated from Québec, Canada. Your information may be stored and processed in Canada or in other countries where Ruffus's service providers operate, including the United States. For EU users, Ruffus relies on appropriate safeguards such as **Standard Contractual Clauses** when transferring data outside the EEA.

## Children

Ruffus is not directed at children under the age of 14. If you are under 14, parental or guardian consent is required before using the service.
