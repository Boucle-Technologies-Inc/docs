Every Ruffus dashboard has a support centre at **Dashboard → Support**. There are two ways in, and you can use whichever suits the question.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Ask the assistant" icon="comments">
    The chat bubble in the corner of your dashboard answers setup and how-to questions straight from this documentation. If it can't help, it hands the conversation over to a ticket — with the transcript attached, so you don't retype anything.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Open a ticket directly" icon="ticket">
    Already know what's wrong? Fill in the form on the support page. No chat required.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The support centre is **owner-only**. Managers land on Card Studio instead — they don't see your tickets.
</Note>

## Opening a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Give it a subject, pick a category (billing, stamps, wallet, NFC, account, bug, or other) and an urgency, then describe what's happening and what you already tried.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the suggested articles">
    As you type a subject, Ruffus suggests up to three documentation pages that look like they answer it. If one of them solves the problem, you've saved yourself the wait. If it doesn't, carry on — the suggestions never block a submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Your ticket gets a reference like **RUF-K7M2QP** and appears in your list straight away. The team is notified by email at the same time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  References deliberately leave out `0`, `O`, `1`, and `I` — they're meant to be read aloud over the phone and typed back without ambiguity.
</Info>

## Following a ticket

Open any ticket from the list to see the full thread and reply. Every ticket carries one of four statuses:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Received** | Submitted and queued. Nobody has picked it up yet. |
| **Reviewing** | Someone on the team is working on it. |
| **Waiting on you** | We've asked you something and the ticket is paused until you answer. |
| **Resolved** | Closed. |

Replying to a **resolved** ticket reopens it, so you never need to file a second ticket for the same issue. Every other status change is made by the support team.

When the team replies, an email goes to the address that opened the ticket — you don't have to keep the dashboard open.

## What the assistant can see

The assistant answers from this documentation site and from what you type. It has no access to your customer list, your stamps, or your billing details — if a question needs those, it will tell you to open a ticket so a human can look.

Chat transcripts are recorded so an escalated conversation arrives with its context intact. **Conversations that never become a ticket are deleted after 90 days.** Transcripts attached to a ticket live as long as the ticket does.

## When to email instead

Tickets are the fastest route for anything about your own account, because they arrive with your merchant details already attached. Email still works for everything else — press, partnerships, or a question from someone without a dashboard login. See [Contact](/help/contact).
