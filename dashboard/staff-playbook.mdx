A loyalty programme fails quietly when nobody at the counter mentions it. The base sits there, no one says anything, no one taps — and the programme looks like it doesn't work when it was simply never introduced.

The playbook exists to stop that. It's the same content in two places: a sheet you print and tape up, and a screen your staff already have open.

  Both read from one source, so the wall and the till can't drift apart. The reward line fills in your real threshold and reward — "Collect 8 stamps, get: free coffee" — rather than a placeholder.
</Note>

## What it covers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A customer's first visit">
    They unlock their phone, hold it to the base for about three seconds, open the link that appears, and sign in once. The first stamp lands, and they're offered Apple or Google Wallet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Every visit after that">
    Unlock, home screen, hold the phone to the base. The card opens and the stamp is added. If they saved it to their Wallet, they can check their count any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A full card">
    A full card stops collecting — the full card **is** the reward. The customer taps the base exactly like any other visit, their full card opens, and they confirm the reward themselves on their own phone. Their screen shows the reward name and a countdown; staff read it and hand the reward over. Nothing to type, nothing to scan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What to actually say">
    Four one-liners staff can use verbatim, including "No app needed — just unlock your phone and tap here" and "Card full? Tap here like usual, confirm on your phone, and I'll hand it over."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where to put the base">
    On the counter where customers pay or order, plainly visible, not behind the till and not buried between other items.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When a tap doesn't work">
    The phone has to be unlocked and on the home screen, and the Wallet app must not already be open. If it still won't read, the customer can scan your QR poster instead and the stamp request lands on the terminal for a staff member to approve.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The one exception worth knowing

A QR poster scan is **not** proof the customer is standing at your counter, so the poster can start a card and earn stamps, but it can't redeem one. **A full card is redeemed only by tapping the base or scanning a till or tablet sticker.** If a customer with a full card can't tap, that's the moment to check the base is powered and reachable — the poster won't offer them the reward.

## Getting there

**Staff playbook** is its own item in the sidebar's **Tools** group, and onboarding links to it once your card is set up. Print it at the same time as your QR poster — the poster goes on the customer's side of the counter, the playbook on yours.
