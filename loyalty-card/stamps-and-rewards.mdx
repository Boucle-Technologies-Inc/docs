Every stamp in Ruffus starts as a request from the customer and only lands once a staff member approves it. That one-tap approval is the whole fraud-prevention model — it's what keeps rewards going only to real visits, not a limitation to work around.

## How a customer earns a stamp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer scans the QR code">
    They point their phone camera at your poster and open the join link in their browser. No app to download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer signs in">
    On their first visit, they create a lightweight account with a phone number (SMS code) or Google/Apple sign-in — no password to choose, and no app download. Returning customers are recognized automatically; everything happens in the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A stamp request is created">
    The customer sees a live countdown. This request stays open for about 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff gets a push notification">
    Whoever is holding the staff device — phone, tablet, or the counter computer — gets an instant push notification with the pending request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff approves in one tap">
    Staff taps **Approve**. The stamp lands on the customer's card immediately and their screen updates in real time. Staff can also **Reject** a request that doesn't look right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If staff don't act within the ~30-second window, the request simply expires. The customer can scan again to start a new one — nothing is lost.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Some locations use an **NFC tap card** at the counter instead of a phone-scanned QR code. A tap grants the stamp instantly with no staff approval step — the printed QR poster still works as a fallback. See [NFC Station Cards](/loyalty-card/nfc-cards) if this is available at your location.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  This flow is for customers standing at your counter. For customers who order through a delivery marketplace or your online store — where you never get their contact details — **printable stamp codes** let them join and stamp from a single-use code dropped in their bag, also with no staff approval step. See [Printable Stamp Codes](/loyalty-card/printable-stamp-codes).
</Tip>

## Cooldown between stamps

To prevent the same visit from generating multiple stamps, Ruffus enforces a cooldown after each stamp is earned — 10 minutes by default. Merchants can adjust or disable this in **Settings → Program rules**. If a customer scans again before the cooldown ends, they see a friendly screen letting them know when they can earn their next stamp.

## How redemption works

A full card holding an unredeemed reward _is_ the reward — there's nothing separate to claim. Opening the card from home, in Wallet or a browser tab, just shows the full card with no button; redeeming only becomes possible once the customer proves they're actually at your counter.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Card fills up">
    Once a customer reaches the required stamp count, their card stops there. No more stamps land on it until it's redeemed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer proves they're at the counter">
    They tap your NFC station card, or scan the QR sticker on one of your tills or tablets — a device-bound QR that only works in your store. Opening the card from home does nothing; one of these two is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redeem now appears">
    Once they've tapped or scanned, their full card shows a **Redeem now** button. The customer taps it and confirms themselves — there's no code for staff to type and nothing to scan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff hold the button">
    Confirming puts a **Hold to validate** button on the customer's screen. A staff member presses and holds it for about a second and a half, on the customer's own phone. A screenshot can't be held down, which is what makes the gesture proof rather than decoration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reward is marked redeemed">
    The button reads **Validated**, the stamp count resets, and a new cycle starts right away. Nothing is deducted until the hold completes — if the reward sits unvalidated for two minutes the window closes, and the customer taps again to restart.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can review redemption activity anytime at **Home → Activity**, which shows KPIs, full redemption history, and your top redeemers alongside stamps and approvals — "Rewards" is no longer its own destination.

<Tip>
  Want to reward by dollars spent or specific products instead of just stamp count? See [Points](/loyalty-card/points) — an optional layer you can run alongside your stamp card (not available during the initial pilot).
</Tip>

## Inactivity expiry (optional)

By default, a customer's balance never expires. From **Settings → Program rules**, you can optionally turn on inactivity expiry: after a long stretch with no visit at all, a customer's whole balance clears — stamps and an unredeemed full card alike. Any visit resets the clock, so an active customer is never at risk. Turning it on isn't retroactive: every current member gets a fresh full window starting the day you enable it, so nobody is cleared for time that already passed. Ruffus sends the customer a warning email before anything clears. See [Campaigns & Automated Messages](/dashboard/campaigns#inactivity-expiry-and-its-warning-email) for how the reminder works.

## Editing your reward

You can rename or re-describe your reward — including updating the French, English, and Spanish labels — at any time from **My cards → Card Studio**. Editing a reward's name or description never affects customer progress; everyone keeps the stamps they've already earned.

## Keeping stamps honest

Ruffus doesn't rely on tricks like hidden watermarks or cryptographic scan signatures to catch fraud — it relies on a simpler, sturdier model:

- Every stamp requires a staff member to approve it, in person, in the moment.
- The cooldown window prevents rapid repeat scans from the same visit.
- Stamp requests are verified server-side and expire automatically if unapproved.
- A screenshot or a replay of the QR code does nothing on its own — it still has to go through a live approval from staff holding the device.

This does mean staff have a small role to play: one tap per stamp. In exchange, every stamp on every card reflects a real visit.

## What if...

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A customer loses their progress?">
    Progress is tied to their account, not their device. If they sign in again — even on a new phone — their stamp history and current progress come right back. If they've added the card to Apple or Google Wallet, that pass also keeps their progress. If something still looks wrong, they (or you, on their behalf) can reach out to [hello@ruffus.app](mailto:hello@ruffus.app).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A customer tries to redeem the same reward twice?">
    It isn't possible. Once staff complete the hold, the stamp count resets and the card is no longer full — there's no separate code to reuse. A screenshot doesn't work either: what staff act on is a button that has to be physically held down, and a screenshot can't be held.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A stamp didn't register after a scan?">
    The most common causes are the cooldown window (they may need to wait before their next stamp is eligible) or the 30-second approval window expiring before staff could act. Have them scan again to start a fresh request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A reward gets redeemed by mistake?">
    There's currently no self-serve way to reverse a redemption. Contact [hello@ruffus.app](mailto:hello@ruffus.app) and the team can help sort it out.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
