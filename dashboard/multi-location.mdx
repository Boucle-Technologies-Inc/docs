Ruffus supports running one loyalty program across multiple physical locations, with per-location and consolidated views. The plan includes 1 store; every store beyond that is an add-on, uncapped.

## Stores on the plan

| | Included | Extra stores | Counter stations per store |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ruffus — \$49.99 CAD/mo | 1 | \$25 CAD/mo each, **no limit** | 3 |

The plan does not run out: past the 1 included store you keep adding them at \$25 CAD/mo apiece, however many you need — a 2-store shop and a 50-store chain are on the same plan, at the same per-store rate. There is no higher plan to graduate to — if you also need a guaranteed SLA, a custom integration or a dedicated contact, that's arranged on top of the plan rather than sold as a separate tier.

<Note>
  "Counter stations per store" is the rate the plan is built on, not a hard per-counter cap. Stations actually draw from one merchant-wide pool — 3 × your store count, plus any purchased station add-ons — so they can sit unevenly across your stores (more devices at a busy flagship, fewer at a quiet second location) rather than being locked to exactly 3 per counter.
</Note>

Extra stores beyond the 1 included are sold as an add-on, billed as an extra line item on your subscription — see [Add-ons](/billing/plans#add-ons) for current rates.

## Adding a location

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a store add-on if needed">
    If you're at your store limit, buy an extra-store add-on from **Dashboard → Billing** — uncapped, so there's always room. Add-on purchases take effect immediately, with prorated billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the location">
    Go to **Settings → Locations → Add**, and fill in the new location's details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up its staff portal">
    Each location gets its own staff portal QR code from the welcome package, so stamp requests route to the right counter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Location filter on your overview

Once you have two or more locations, a location filter appears on the **Dashboard** overview screen, letting you switch between a **consolidated** view (all locations combined) and a **per-location** view, with per-location analytics breakdowns in addition to the consolidated numbers.

## Staff portals and QR variants

Every location has its own staff portal and staff QR code — staff at one location approve stamp requests for that location, not others. If a location has multiple staff devices working the counter at once, each device gets its own QR variant so stamp-request push notifications route to the right device instead of all of them at once.

There's no email-invite flow here either — staff access is entirely PIN-based, set up per staff member at **Dashboard → Staff**.

## Unified vs. granular mode

Under **Settings**, you can choose how tightly your locations are managed:

- **Unified mode** — program rules, card design, and settings apply the same way across every location.
- **Granular mode** — individual locations can diverge where it makes sense (for example, different program-rule tweaks per site).

## Dropping below your store count

If you drop store add-ons and the result can't cover your current stores, you'll be asked to choose which locations stay active. The others are deactivated, not deleted — their data is preserved if you add stores back later.

Growing has no equivalent limit: since extra stores are uncapped, adding your eleventh — or your five hundredth — is always just another add-on purchase.

## Beyond the plan

There is no plan above Ruffus's single plan. What some large operators do arrange separately is a guaranteed SLA, a custom integration, custom wallet certificates, or a dedicated contact — negotiated on top of a Ruffus subscription rather than sold as another tier.
