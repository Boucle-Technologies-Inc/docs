**Settings** is where the rest of your program's configuration lives — anything not covered by [Multi-Location](/dashboard/multi-location), [Points](/loyalty-card/points), [VIP Tiers](/loyalty-card/tiers), [Nearby Reminders](/loyalty-card/nearby-reminders), or [Campaigns](/dashboard/campaigns). All of it is owner-only.

## White-label domain

Pick a subdomain for your dashboard — `yourshop.ruffus.app` instead of the default — from **Settings**. This is cosmetic: it changes the URL your staff and you use to reach the dashboard, not where customer-facing card links live.

## Program rules

**Program rules** (per card, if you run more than one) bundles the settings that control day-to-day stamping and redemption behaviour:

- **Anti-fraud delay** — the minimum gap between stamp requests from the same customer at the same location, so one visit can't be stamped twice. **10 minutes** on the recommended settings every new account starts with; switch to custom mode to set your own length or turn it off entirely.
- **Inactivity expiry** — off by default, so a dormant balance never clears. Turn it on and pick a window, in months, after which a customer's entire balance — stamps and an unredeemed full card alike — clears if they haven't visited at all in that window. Any visit resets the clock, and turning the setting on for the first time gives every current member a fresh window starting that day, so nobody is cleared for time that already passed.
- **Require staff PIN** and the merchant email toggles below also live in this same panel.

## Staff PIN

Every stamp still requires a staff member to tap approve in the staff portal — the fraud-prevention model described in [How It Works](/how-it-works), and that approval tap can't be turned off. **Require staff PIN** is a narrower, separate toggle: whether staff must also enter their own PIN before stamping or redeeming.

A brand-new merchant starts with this **off**, so the welcome-package staff QR works immediately without setting up PINs first. Turn it **on** once you've added real staff, for per-staff attribution and PIN protection on stamping and bulk stamps.

<Warning>
  Turning Require staff PIN off does not skip the approval tap — a staff member still has to approve every stamp request. It only removes the PIN step and per-staff attribution layered on top of that approval; anyone with access to the staff screen can then stamp without entering a PIN.
</Warning>

## Merchant email preferences

Two more toggles live in the same Program rules panel and control emails Ruffus sends **you**, not your customers — both are CASL-relevant opt-outs, on by default:

- **Milestone emails** — an occasional celebration email when your program hits a milestone (returning customers, total members, or stamps given).
- **Monthly recap email** — a monthly summary of your program: stamps issued, new members, rewards redeemed, and who's close to a reward.

See [Campaigns & Automated Messages](/dashboard/campaigns) for the customer-facing lifecycle emails, which are separate from these two.

## Fraud monitoring

Ruffus watches for unusual patterns — things like implausible location jumps between scans or unusually fast repeat activity — and surfaces a flag on your dashboard when something looks off, so you can take a closer look. This runs automatically in the background; there's nothing to configure.

## Referrals

Turn on **customer referrals** to reward customers who bring in a friend — both the referrer and the new member get a bonus when the friend joins, at an amount you set.

<Warning>
  **Customer referrals are not available during the initial pilot.** This toggle is disabled while we onboard the first wave of merchants stamps-only. Email [hello@ruffus.app](mailto:hello@ruffus.app) if this is important to your program. This does not affect **Refer a Business** below, which stays open.
</Warning>

<Note>
  This is separate from **Refer a Business** (also under Settings), which is how you refer other merchants to Ruffus itself — not part of your customer-facing loyalty program. See your dashboard for current referral terms.
</Note>

## Language

Set your own dashboard language independently of the trilingual FR/EN/ES program your customers see — the two aren't linked.
