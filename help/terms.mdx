## Account security

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure. Do not share your password or terminal access with anyone outside your own staff. Counter staff never get a dashboard login — they use 6-digit PINs you create at **Dashboard → Staff**, which is the whole access model; there is no email-invite flow. If you suspect unauthorised access, change your password immediately and notify the team at [hello@ruffus.app](mailto:hello@ruffus.app).

Stamps and rewards issued through Ruffus have **no monetary value** and cannot be exchanged for cash, credit, or any other form of currency. They exist solely within the Ruffus loyalty programme as a benefit defined by each merchant. Ruffus is not responsible for any merchant's decision about what reward to offer or how to fulfil it.

## Programme changes by merchants

Merchants may modify their loyalty program — including the stamp count, reward description, or card design — at any time. Customer progress is always preserved: changing the stamp count never resets stamps a customer has already earned, and lowering the goal retroactively grants rewards to customers who newly qualify.

## Cancellation and account deletion

You can cancel your Ruffus subscription at any time from your dashboard's **Billing** page. Cancelling stops future renewals, but your account remains active until the end of the billing period you've already paid for. After cancellation, loyalty data is retained for a 30-day grace period and then permanently deleted — see the [Privacy Policy](/help/privacy) for details. To permanently delete your account and request removal of your personal data, email [privacy@ruffus.app](mailto:privacy@ruffus.app). Deletion requests are processed in accordance with the [Ruffus Privacy Policy](/help/privacy).

## Seasonal pause

If your business closes for part of the year, you can pause your subscription instead of cancelling it, from **Dashboard → Billing**.

- **One pause every 12 months**, running up to **4 consecutive months**. It lifts automatically at the end, and you can resume earlier at any time.
- **Billing stops** and no invoice is raised for the paused period.
- **Your loyalty program is suspended too** — customers can't join, earn stamps or redeem rewards until you resume.
- **Nothing is lost.** Members, stamps, history and your NFC cards are all retained, and pausing opens no card-return window.
- **A pause neither extends nor restarts a free trial.**

## NFC station cards are loaned, not sold

The NFC tap cards that ship with your plan stay Ruffus property the whole time you use them — active, seasonally paused, or closed. Nothing about them is a purchase.

- **If you cancel or your account closes**, return the cards you hold within **30 days**. We email you a **prepaid return label at no cost**, plus reminders during the window.
- **If you move to a plan with fewer locations**, the same 30 days, label and reminders apply to the cards at the location you dropped. You keep the cards at the locations you keep. The window opens when the plan change takes effect — at the end of your current billing period on a paid plan, immediately if you're still in your free trial.
- **A card not returned in the window costs $20 CAD**, charged to the payment method on file (retried over the following days, and written off if it can't be collected). The same $20 CAD per card applies to a damaged, lost, or replacement card at any time.
- **A seasonal pause is not a return event** — pause your account for the off-season and keep your cards.
- **Your trial ending never triggers a subscription charge.** Your account pauses instead; the card on file is only ever charged the fees described here.
