Every loyalty card in Ruffus is designed in **Card Studio**, found at **Dashboard → Card Studio**. This is where you control how your card looks and how many stamps it takes to earn a reward. Changes are owner-only and apply immediately — no publishing step, no waiting.

The studio centres on a live preview of the card itself — the exact card your customers see when they tap your NFC station card or scan your QR code — and it updates in real time as you edit. Use the **Preview the tap** button to play the same stamp animation and confetti celebration your customers get at the counter. The printable QR poster and the Apple/Google Wallet previews sit in tabs beside the card preview.

<Note>
  Only the account owner can edit card design. Managers and staff can view the card but cannot change its appearance or reward rules.
</Note>

## What you can customize

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Card style" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Choose how the whole card is painted: **Gradient**, **Photo**, or **Solid**. See [Card style](#card-style) below.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Logo" icon="image">
    Upload your business logo. It appears at the top of the customer card, on the QR poster, and inside the Apple and Google Wallet passes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Brand colour" icon="palette">
    Pick a brand colour — it becomes the card's face colour and carries through the QR poster and the wallet pass accents. Light or dark, text and stamps adjust automatically to stay readable.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Card image" icon="panorama">
    Add a background image for the card — a shot of your space, your product, or anything that reflects your brand.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Stamp shape" icon="shapes">
    Choose the shape each stamp is drawn in: circle, square, triangle, diamond, star, hexagon, or heart.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Stamp fill" icon="brush">
    Set the fill style, icon, and colour used for earned stamps so they match your brand at a glance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Progress display" icon="chart-simple">
    Show progress as individual **stamps** or as a single **progress bar**.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Stamp count" icon="grid-2">
    Decide how many stamps a customer needs to collect before their reward unlocks.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Fill style and icon only apply to **circle** stamps. Pick any other shape — square, triangle, diamond, star, hexagon, or heart — and earned stamps always render as a plain solid fill in your stamp colour, regardless of the fill style or icon you've chosen. If you want a checkmark or an icon on your earned stamps, keep the stamp shape set to circle.
</Note>

## Card style

**Card style** decides how the whole card is painted. It's the first choice worth making, because the rest of the settings sit on top of it.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Gradient" icon="circle-half-stroke">
    The default. Your brand colour is deepened into a gradient, and earned stamps are struck like small metal coins pressed into the card.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Photo" icon="camera">
    Your own photo, with the card's details on a frosted glass panel over it. No photo yet? The card shows a slow drift of colour drawn from your brand colour instead.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Solid" icon="square">
    One flat colour, edge to edge. The simplest option, and the one to pick if you want nothing competing with your logo.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  **Photo** works best with a wide shot that has some empty space in the upper half — the panel covers the bottom of the card, and your business name sits over the top. A busy photo still reads fine: Ruffus measures your image and adjusts the panel and the text so they stay legible on it, whether the photo is dark or pale.
</Tip>

Your brand colour still matters on every style. On **Gradient** it becomes the card face, on **Photo** it colours the drift behind the photo, and on all three it carries through to your Apple and Google Wallet passes.

## Progress display

By default a card shows one stamp per visit, in the shape you chose. Switching to a **progress bar** replaces the grid with a single bar that fills as customers earn.

A bar suits high targets — a card that takes twenty visits shows twenty small stamps, which get hard to read on a phone. It also suits goals that never divided cleanly into stamps in the first place.

<Note>
  Stamp shape, fill style, and icon apply to the stamp grid only. Choose the progress bar and those controls are hidden, because there are no individual stamps left to mark.
</Note>

## Reward label in three languages

Ruffus is trilingual by default. When you set your reward — for example "Free coffee" — you write it once for each of French, English, and Spanish. Ruffus shows the customer the version that matches their chosen language automatically, on the card, in wallet passes, and in emails.

<Tip>
  Keep the reward label short and concrete. "Café gratuit," "Free coffee," and "Café gratis" all read cleanly on a small stamp card — long descriptions get cut off on some phone screens.
</Tip>

## Changes apply immediately

As soon as you save a change in Card Studio, it takes effect for every customer:

- The customer-facing card updates the next time it loads.
- Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes already on customers' phones refresh automatically to reflect the new design.
- The QR poster reflects the new branding the next time you download or print it.

## Changing your stamp count

You can raise or lower the number of stamps required for a reward at any time. This never resets customer progress:

- **Raising the count** — customers keep every stamp they've already earned and simply need a few more to reach the new total.
- **Lowering the count** — any customer whose existing stamp total now meets or exceeds the new, lower threshold is retroactively granted their reward.

<Note>
  There's no "undo" on a stamp-count change beyond adjusting the number again — but because progress is never lost either direction, it's safe to experiment until the card feels right.
</Note>

## Running more than one card

Ruffus supports multiple loyalty cards side by side — each with its own name, stamp count, and reward label — managed from **Dashboard → Cards**. Your Card Studio branding (logo, colours, stamp style) is shared across all of them. See [Multiple Cards](/loyalty-card/multiple-cards) for how caps, enrollment, and stamping work.

For a limited-time promotion, such as bonus stamps for new members or double stamps at a specific location, you don't need a separate card — use **Campaigns**, which can be scoped by location, date range, and card.
