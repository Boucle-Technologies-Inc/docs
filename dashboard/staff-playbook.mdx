A loyalty programme fails quietly when nobody at the counter mentions it. The base sits there, no one says anything, no one taps — and the programme looks like it doesn't work when it was simply never introduced.

The playbook exists to stop that. It's the same content in two places: a sheet you print and tape up, and a screen your staff already have open.

## Three ways a stamp happens

The terminal is a guest on hardware another POS owns, so the counter isn't one flow with fallbacks — it's three, and a store picks by what hardware it has. The playbook opens with all three so a new staff member knows which one is yours.

| Mode | When it applies | What staff do |
|---|---|---|
| **Tap the base** | A base is out on your counter. | Nothing — the customer taps it and the stamp lands on its own. |
| **Show the code on your screen** | No base out, or it isn't responding. | Open the terminal, tap the QR code to enlarge it, turn the screen to the customer. Their scan closes the code and shows you **Approve**. |
| **The printed poster** | A poster is up where customers can reach it. | They scan it themselves. Keep the terminal open, or turn notifications on, so you see the request in time. |

Only the third needs a staff approval you have to notice, because it's the only one with no structural proof the customer is standing there. The first two put the customer in front of you by construction.

## What it covers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A customer's first visit">
    They unlock their phone, hold it to the base for about three seconds, open the link that appears, and sign in once. The first stamp lands, and they're offered Apple or Google Wallet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Every visit after that">
    Unlock, home screen, hold the phone to the base. The card opens and the stamp is added. If they saved it to their Wallet, they can check their count any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A full card">
    A full card stops collecting — the full card **is** the reward. The customer taps the base exactly like any other visit, their full card opens, and they confirm the reward themselves on their own phone. A **Hold to validate** button then appears on their screen: a staff member presses and holds it for about a second and a half, and it reads **Validated**. There's no code to type and nothing to scan, but the hold is the step that actually releases the reward — hold it, then hand the reward over. If it sits longer than two minutes the window closes and the customer taps the base again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What to actually say">
    Four one-liners staff can use verbatim, including "No app needed — just unlock your phone and tap here" and "Card full? Tap here like usual, confirm on your phone, and I'll hand it over."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where to put the base">
    On the counter where customers pay or order, plainly visible, not behind the till and not buried between other items.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When a tap doesn't work">
    The phone has to be unlocked and on the home screen, and the Wallet app must not already be open. If it still won't read, the customer can scan your QR poster instead and the stamp request lands on the terminal for a staff member to approve.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The one exception worth knowing

A QR poster scan is **not** proof the customer is standing at your counter, so the poster can start a card and earn stamps, but it can't redeem one. **A full card is redeemed only by tapping the base or scanning a till or tablet sticker.** If a customer with a full card can't tap, that's the moment to check the base is powered and reachable — the poster won't offer them the reward.

## Getting there

**Staff playbook** is a tab under **In store**, and onboarding links to it once your card is set up. Print it at the same time as your QR poster — the poster goes on the customer's side of the counter, the playbook on yours.
