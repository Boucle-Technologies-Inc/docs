Managing your Ruffus subscription should be straightforward. Below you will find answers to the most common billing questions. If you cannot find what you are looking for, reach out to the team directly at [billing@ruffus.app](mailto:billing@ruffus.app).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?">
    No card to start. You add a payment method in two situations: before your free NFC tap cards ship (that card is held as a deposit for the card fees, never charged for a subscription), and when you pick a paid plan to continue after the trial.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When does my free trial actually start?">
    At your **first real stamp**, not at sign-up. Setting up, branding your card and testing the dashboard cost you no trial days — the 14 days begin the first time a customer earns a stamp, from any source (QR, NFC tap, staff grant or a connected store). Imported stamps and Practice Mode rehearsals never start the clock. An account that never records a stamp is closed after 90 days, with a warning email first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. Ruffus is a no-contract service, so you can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard's **Billing** page. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged again after that. If you change your mind before the period ends, you can reactivate from the same page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I close for the off-season — do I have to cancel?">
    No. Pause instead, from **Dashboard → Billing → Pause for the season**. Billing stops and no invoice is raised for the paused months, and your members, stamps, history and NFC tap cards stay exactly as you left them — a pause never opens a card-return window. Your loyalty program pauses along with the billing, so customers can't join, earn or redeem while you're closed. A pause runs up to **4 consecutive months** and lifts automatically, or you can resume earlier in one click; the allowance is **one pause every 12 months**. See [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans#seasonal-pause).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a long-term contract?">
    No. Ruffus operates on a month-to-month basis — there is no long-term commitment. If you choose annual billing to take advantage of 2 months free, your subscription runs for one year, but you are never locked in beyond that billing cycle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does annual billing work?">
    When you switch to annual billing, Ruffus gives you **2 months free** — an instant saving compared to paying monthly. The full amount is billed upfront at the start of each billing year. To switch, go to **Billing** in your dashboard and select the annual option for your plan. Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately, with the unused portion of your current period prorated on the invoice. Switching back from annual to monthly is treated as a downgrade instead: it's scheduled for the end of your current billing period rather than applied right away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?">
    Open **Billing** in your dashboard and select **Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately, with the difference prorated on your next invoice. Downgrades are scheduled for the end of your current billing period — you keep your current plan until then, and the new price applies from your next renewal. You can cancel a scheduled downgrade from **Billing** any time before it takes effect. If you downgrade below your current number of active locations, you'll choose which locations stay active; the others are deactivated at renewal (not deleted).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans mid-month?">
    Yes. You can switch plans at any time — you are not locked in until the end of a billing cycle. If you upgrade mid-month, Ruffus prorates the charge so you only pay for the remainder of the current period at the new rate. If you downgrade, you keep your current plan through the end of the period you've already paid for, and the lower rate kicks in at the start of your next billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer refunds?">
    We don't refund partial billing periods, except where required by law. Cancelling stops the next renewal rather than refunding time already paid for. If something went wrong on our end, we'll always make it right — contact the billing team at [billing@ruffus.app](mailto:billing@ruffus.app) with your account email and a description of the issue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    Ruffus accepts major credit and debit cards, processed securely by Stripe. If you have specific payment requirements — such as invoicing rather than card-on-file for a large annual subscription — contact [billing@ruffus.app](mailto:billing@ruffus.app) to discuss your options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I update my billing information?">
    Go to **Billing** in your dashboard and select **Payment method**. From there you can add a new card, update an existing one, or change the card used for future charges. Updates take effect immediately and apply to your next scheduled payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a payment fails?">
    If a renewal charge fails — for example, an expired or declined card — your loyalty program is paused: stamps, redemptions, and campaigns are unavailable until billing is resolved. Your data and your customers' cards are never deleted. Update your card under **Billing → Payment method** in your dashboard; once the payment goes through, everything resumes automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when my trial ends?">
    **Nothing is auto-charged.** If you picked a plan and added a payment method, billing simply begins. If you did not, your subscription pauses instead of being charged: stamping, joining, redemptions and campaigns stop until you choose a plan and add a payment method from your dashboard's **Billing** page. Nothing is deleted when the pause happens, and resuming restores everything as it was.

    The card you added so we could ship your NFC tap cards does not count here — it is held as a deposit for card fees only and is never used to start a subscription.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
