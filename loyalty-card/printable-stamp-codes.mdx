Delivery marketplaces like Uber Eats, DoorDash, and SkipTheDishes don't share a customer's name, phone number, or email with you — so a customer who only ever orders through one of them can never become a member the normal way. Printable stamp codes solve this with paper instead of an integration: you generate a sheet of single-use QR codes, print it on your own printer, cut the codes apart, and drop one into every bag or box you send out. A customer who scans it joins your program (if they aren't already a member) and collects a stamp — no app, no POS, and no data ever flows from the marketplace to Ruffus.

<Note>
  Ruffus doesn't integrate with Uber Eats, DoorDash, or any other marketplace, and never ingests their order data. The printed code is the entire mechanism — it's what makes this work without an integration at all.
</Note>

## Generating a batch

From **In store → Stamp codes**, generate a new batch:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a store and card">
    Choose which location and which loyalty card the codes should stamp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">
    Label the batch **Delivery**, **Online store**, **Catering**, **Event**, or **Other** — this is for your own reporting only and never changes how a code behaves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a number of sheets">
    Each sheet prints **24 codes** on a US Letter page, up to a maximum of **20 sheets (480 codes) per batch**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate & download">
    Ruffus mints the batch and immediately downloads a PDF — one page per sheet, with dashed cut lines around each code so you can cut them apart cleanly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Give the batch a label you'll recognize later, like "Uber Eats — August." It's what identifies the batch in your batch history, so make it specific enough to tell batches apart.
</Tip>

Generating and voiding batches is **owner-only**. Managers can view the batch history from **In store → Stamp codes** but won't see the generate form.

## What happens when a customer scans

<Steps>
  <Step title="They scan the code">
    No app needed — it opens straight in their phone's camera or browser, the same as your counter QR poster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They sign in or join">
    Returning members are recognized automatically. A first-time scanner creates a lightweight account with a phone number (SMS code) or Google/Apple sign-in, the same as joining from your counter poster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The stamp lands immediately">
    Unlike a counter scan, there's no staff approval step. The code itself is the credential — it's destroyed the instant it's used, so there's nothing left for staff to verify.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each code works **exactly once**. A customer who scans a code that's already been used sees a clear "already used" screen — it can't be reused, shared, or claimed a second time by anyone.
</Note>

Codes don't expire on a clock. A code stays valid until it's either scanned or its batch is voided — so a sheet sitting in a drawer for months still works when you finally use it.

## Voiding a batch

If a printed stack is lost or stolen, void the batch from its row in the batch table. Voiding immediately:

- Kills every **unredeemed** code in that batch — a scan of any of them fails from that moment on.
- Blocks the batch's PDF from ever being re-downloaded.

<Warning>
  Voiding is **irreversible** — there's no un-void. It's also **owner-only**. Stamps a customer already collected from that batch are unaffected; voiding only stops codes that haven't been scanned yet.
</Warning>

## Re-downloading a sheet

Lost the PDF, or need another copy for a second printer? Click **Re-download** on any active batch's row to get the exact same sheet again, any time. A voided batch can't be re-downloaded — that's part of what voiding is for.

## Tracking performance

Each batch's row shows how many codes were **generated**, how many have been **redeemed**, and the resulting **redemption rate**, so you can gauge how many of your delivery or online orders are converting into members. Since Ruffus never sees marketplace order data, this is the whole picture Ruffus can offer — compare it against your own marketplace dashboard's order count to see your true conversion rate.

## What if...

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A customer scans a code from a voided batch?">
    They see a clear "this code is no longer valid" screen. Nothing is granted, and there's no way to salvage an individual code from a voided batch — the batch has to be regenerated.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two customers scan the same code at the same time?">
    Only one wins. The code can only ever be claimed once, so whichever scan lands first gets the stamp and the second sees the "already used" screen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A customer isn't enrolled in the card the batch was printed for?">
    They're guided through joining first, exactly like a first counter visit. Once they finish joining, the stamp is applied automatically — nothing is lost by not being a member yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reuse a batch for a different channel later?">
    Not directly — a batch is generated against one card and one channel label at print time. Generate a new batch with the channel you need; it costs nothing but paper.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
