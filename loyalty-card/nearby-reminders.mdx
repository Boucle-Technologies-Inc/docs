Apple Wallet and Google Wallet can both surface a customer's loyalty card on their lock screen when they're physically near your shop — a gentle nudge to stop in and collect a stamp. Ruffus turns this on automatically for every card; switch it off per card if you'd rather not use it.

## Turning it off (or back on)

The **Nearby reminder** toggle lives in **Settings → Program rules**, per card. It's **on by default** — turn it off for any card where you don't want the reminder.

<Note>
  There's nothing for the customer to set up. If they've already added the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, the reminder activates automatically the next time their pass refreshes.
</Note>

## What to expect

- **Works on Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.** Both platforms use the same store location(s) and the same per-card toggle — Ruffus doesn't treat one platform differently from the other.
- **Uses your store's address.** Ruffus attaches the location(s) you have on file to the pass so the wallet app knows where to trigger the reminder.
- **The message differs by platform.** Apple shows a lock-screen line naming your business (something like "*[Your business]* is nearby — your card is ready"). Google Wallet controls its own notification wording, so Ruffus can't customize what Android shows.
- **Not available on the home-screen PWA.** This is a wallet-pass feature — customers who saved their card to their home screen instead of Apple or Google Wallet (see [Wallet](/loyalty-card/wallet)) won't get it.
- **Changes apply on the next refresh, not instantly.** Turning the toggle on or off doesn't push immediately to passes already sitting in a customer's wallet — it takes effect the next time each pass refreshes, such as after their next stamp.

See [Multi-Location](/dashboard/multi-location) if you need to check or update the addresses on file for your locations, and [Wallet](/loyalty-card/wallet) for how Apple and Google Wallet passes work more generally.
