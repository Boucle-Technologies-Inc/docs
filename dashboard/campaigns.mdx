Ruffus keeps customers engaged in two ways: promos you run yourself from **Campaigns**, and a set of lifecycle messages Ruffus sends automatically, with no setup required. This page covers both.

## Running your own campaigns

From **Campaigns**, you can launch:

- **Bonus stamps** — customers earn extra stamps (up to 12) on top of the normal one per approved stamp request, for a limited time.
- **New-member bonus** — customers who join during the promo window get a stamp-count head start.

- **Wallet push** — a short notification you write yourself, delivered to the lock screens of customers who saved your card to Apple or Google Wallet. See [Wallet push](#wallet-push) below.

All three can be scoped to a specific [location](/dashboard/multi-location) and a specific [card](/loyalty-card/multiple-cards), with a start and end date — handy for a seasonal push without touching your permanent program rules.

<Tip>
  For an always-on reward instead of a limited-time promo, see [VIP Tiers](/loyalty-card/tiers) — tiers grant a standing bonus-stamp perk to loyal customers rather than everyone, for a limited window. Not available during the initial pilot.
</Tip>

## Wallet push

**Wallet push** is the one message you compose yourself. You write a **notification title** and a line of **notification text**, and it reaches customers who saved your card to Apple or Google Wallet — on the lock screen, without an app.

It's deliberately narrow, and the limits are not adjustable:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Four sends a month" icon="calendar">
    Per merchant, on the calendar month, resetting on the 1st. The composer shows **"N of 4 used this month"** before you send.
  </Card>

  <Card title="One a week per customer" icon="user-clock">
    A customer who received any merchant's wallet campaign in the last 7 days is skipped. Someone holding five Ruffus cards does not get five pushes in a week.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Before sending, the composer shows an **estimated recipients** count for the scope you picked, so you can see how narrow a store-or-card filter makes the send.

<Note>
  **Who receives it.** Only customers whose pass is still installed. Saving your card to a wallet is what opts a customer in, so there's no separate list to build — but because deleting the pass would also cost them their stamps, customers can switch merchant marketing off from their own card's account screen without giving up the card. Those customers are excluded too.
</Note>

<Warning>
  A paused card can't be targeted. Resume it first, or scope the campaign to a card that's running.
</Warning>

## Automatic lifecycle messages

Ruffus also sends a set of automatic emails and push notifications on your behalf — nothing to build, nothing to send. Most aren't configurable; two are.

| Message | Sent when | Frequency | Configurable? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | A customer joins your card | Once, on joining | No |
| First stamp | A customer earns their very first stamp | Once | No |
| Milestone | A customer crosses a lifetime-stamp milestone (10, 25, 50, 100) | Once per milestone | No |
| [Reward ready](/loyalty-card/wallet) | A customer completes their card | Once per completed card | No |
| Reward reminder | A completed reward sits unclaimed for 14\+ days | Every 7 days, until claimed | Indirectly — shares win-back's personal note |
| Redemption follow-up | Right after a customer redeems a reward | Once per redemption | No |
| Inactivity-expiry warning | A customer's balance is approaching inactivity-expiry clearing (see below) | Once, before it clears | Indirectly — via your inactivity-expiry setting |
| Slow-day nudge | A day of the week you've marked as historically quiet | Weekly, on the day(s) you pick | **Yes** — off by default |
| Anniversary | The anniversary of a customer's join date | Once a year | No |
| Birthday | A customer's self-reported birthday (set from their own account) | Once a year | No |
| Win-back | A customer hasn't visited in 30\+ days | At most once every 60 days | **Yes** — personal note |

All of these send by email and push notification together, same as the rest of Ruffus's customer messaging.

### Win-back's personal note

The one thing you can customize on the win-back message is a short personal note, up to 280 characters, appended to the email. Find it at **Settings → Win-back**, alongside an audience preview and send history — it's a Settings tab, not part of this Campaigns page. See [Customers](/dashboard/customers#win-back-emails-automatic) for the full win-back picture.

The Reward reminder email above uses this same personal-note field — it isn't a separate setting, so whatever you write for win-back also appears on reward reminders.

### Slow-day nudges

Unlike everything else in this table, the slow-day nudge is entirely opt-in: pick which day (or days) of the week tend to be quiet for your business, and Ruffus sends a nudge to recently-active-but-not-regular customers on those days. It's off until you turn it on — there's no default day.

### Inactivity expiry and its warning email

By default, a customer's balance never expires. From **Settings → Program rules**, you can optionally turn on inactivity expiry: after a long stretch with no visit at all, a customer's whole balance clears — stamps and an unredeemed full card alike — and any visit resets the clock. If you turn this on, Ruffus automatically sends the expiration-reminder message above while there's still time for the customer to come back.

<Note>
  Send history for anniversary, expiration, slow-day, and both reward messages is visible from **Settings → Win-back**'s history panel, alongside win-back sends — it's the one place to see recent automated sends across message types.
</Note>

## Messages Ruffus sends you

Everything above is customer-facing. Separate from that, Ruffus also emails **you** (the merchant) automatically:

- **Milestone emails** — a celebration email when your program crosses a milestone rung on returning customers, total members, or stamps issued. At most one per day. On by default.
- **Monthly recap** — a summary of last month's stamps issued, new members, and rewards redeemed, sent early each month. On by default.

Both toggles live in **Settings → Program rules** — see [Program Settings](/dashboard/settings).
