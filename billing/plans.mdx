There is **one Ruffus plan**. It includes the full loyalty toolkit — campaigns, Apple/Google Wallet passes, analytics, the complete API, automatic win-back emails, and your QR poster — with no feature gate. What changes as you grow is how many stores you run, which is a quantity on the plan, not a different plan to buy.

<Note>
  **Points, VIP tiers, and customer referrals are paused for the initial pilot**, independent of plan or billing — Ruffus is launching stamps-only while we onboard the first wave of merchants. Every account keeps its stamp card, campaigns, Wallet passes, analytics, and the full API. See [Points](/loyalty-card/points) and [VIP Tiers](/loyalty-card/tiers) for details, or email [hello@ruffus.app](mailto:hello@ruffus.app) about early access.

## What's included

The plan includes:

- 1 store, expandable to as many as you want
- Up to 4 loyalty cards — stamps, points, and VIP tiers
- 3 counter stations per store, each with its own QR
- Unlimited stamps and members
- Apple Wallet and Google Wallet cards, no app to install
- Full API and webhooks — every scope, no plan gate
- Automatic touchpoints: welcome, birthday, and win-back
- 2 NFC tap cards included per store — one phone tap, one stamp
- Printable QR poster
- Trilingual FR/EN/ES program
- Analytics dashboard

<Note>
  **Need an SLA, a custom integration, or a dedicated contact?** That's Custom — not a fourth plan or a bigger store bracket, a bespoke arrangement layered on top of the plan above, priced by conversation. Email [hello@ruffus.app](mailto:hello@ruffus.app).
</Note>

Ruffus is trilingual out of the box — your card, dashboard, emails, and wallet passes all work in French, English, and Spanish, not just two of the three.

## Add-ons

The plan can add counter stations and stores, as paid add-ons billed as extra line items on the same subscription (same interval as your plan — monthly add-ons on a monthly plan, annual on annual):

- **Extra counter station** — \$5 CAD/mo, per station.
- **Extra store** — \$25 CAD/mo, per store, **uncapped**. A 2-store shop and a 50-store chain are on the same plan, at the same per-store rate.

Add-on quantities carry over automatically when you change your billing cadence (store add-ons are repriced to the new cadence's rate — monthly to annual or back).

## Annual billing

Paying annually gets you **2 months free** compared to paying monthly:

- Plan: \$499.90 CAD/yr (equivalent to \$41.66/mo)
- Extra store add-on: \$250 CAD/yr each (equivalent to \$20.83/mo)

Annual plans are billed upfront for the full year. Switching from monthly to annual billing takes effect immediately, with prorated billing for the switch. Switching from annual back to monthly is treated as a downgrade — see [Changing your billing cadence](#changing-your-billing-cadence) below — so it's scheduled for the end of your current billing period instead.

## Free trial

Every new account gets a **14-day free trial** with full feature access — no card to start.

The trial clock starts at your **first real stamp**, not at sign-up. Setting up your card, branding your poster and testing the dashboard cost you no trial days; the 14 days begin the first time a customer actually earns a stamp, from any source (QR, NFC tap, staff grant or a connected store). Imported stamps and Practice Mode rehearsals never start it.

A payment method is required before your free NFC tap cards ship. That card is held as a deposit for the card fees described in [NFC Cards](/loyalty-card/nfc-cards) — it is not charged for a subscription.

<Warning>
  **We never auto-charge at the end of a trial.** If your trial ends without a payment method on file, your subscription pauses instead: stamping, joining and redemption stop until you add one. Nothing is deleted when this happens. An account that never records a stamp is closed after 90 days, with a warning email first.
</Warning>

## Changing your billing cadence

There's one plan, so there's no plan to upgrade to — the only axis left is how often you're billed, plus however many stores you're running.

**Monthly → annual** takes effect **immediately**: the difference is prorated so you only pay for the remainder of the current period at the new rate.

**Annual → monthly** is **scheduled for the end of your current billing period**. You keep everything you've already paid for until then, and the monthly rate applies from your next renewal. Changed your mind? You can cancel a scheduled cadence change from **Billing** any time before it takes effect.

Loyalty cards are capped at 4, so a cadence change never affects your cards. (A small number of legacy accounts provisioned above this cap may still be asked to choose which cards stay active when their cadence changes.) See [Multiple Cards](/loyalty-card/multiple-cards) for how the cap works.

## Cancelling

You can cancel anytime from **Dashboard → Billing**. Your program stays active through the end of your current paid period — cancelling doesn't cut you off immediately. If you're on an annual plan, cancelling stops the renewal; there's no partial refund for the unused months of the year. You can reactivate anytime before your period ends; after a full cancellation, you'd resubscribe through checkout. Closing your account starts a 30-day window to return your NFC tap cards — see [NFC Cards](/loyalty-card/nfc-cards).

Closing seasonally rather than for good? [Pause instead](#seasonal-pause).

## Seasonal pause

If your business closes for part of the year, pause your subscription instead of cancelling. Open **Dashboard → Billing** and choose **Pause for the season**.

- **Billing stops** for the paused months, and no invoice is raised for that period.
- **Your loyalty program pauses too.** Customers can't join, earn stamps or redeem rewards until you resume — the account is inactive, not running for free.
- **Nothing is lost.** Members, stamps, history, branding and your NFC tap cards all stay exactly as you left them. A pause never opens a card-return window.
- **It ends on its own.** A pause runs for up to **4 consecutive months** and lifts automatically; you can resume earlier in one click from the same page.
- **Once every 12 months.** The allowance is one pause per rolling 12-month period, which is what one off-season means. Past that, cancelling and returning the hardware is the honest option.

A pause neither extends nor restarts a free trial.

We don't refund partial billing periods, except where required by law. If something went wrong on our end, we'll always make it right — reach out to [billing@ruffus.app](mailto:billing@ruffus.app).
