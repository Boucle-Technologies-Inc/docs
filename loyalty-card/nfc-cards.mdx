An NFC station card sits at your counter next to (or instead of) your printed QR poster. A customer taps their phone against it and their stamp lands immediately — no camera, no staff approval step.

<Info>
  Every plan includes **2 free NFC tap cards per location**, shipped pre-programmed within 1–2 business days. You can request them yourself from the dashboard — see [Ordering cards](#ordering-cards) below.
</Info>

## How a tap works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer taps their phone">
    Holding their phone near the card opens a link in their browser — no app, no camera permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ruffus verifies the tap">
    Every tap carries a fresh, single-use cryptographic signature from the card itself. Ruffus checks it's genuine and hasn't been used before, then grants the stamp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stamp lands instantly">
    No staff approval needed — the same cooldown and campaign-bonus rules that apply to QR stamps also apply here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the customer isn't signed in yet, they're guided through a quick sign-up first, then the stamp is applied automatically once they finish joining — nothing is lost.
</Note>

## Managing your cards

From **Dashboard → Card Studio**, an **NFC Cards** section lists every card enrolled at your location(s), lets you revoke a lost or damaged card, and request more.

- **Revoke** takes effect immediately — a revoked card stops working on its next tap, so a lost card is dead the moment you say so.
- Cards are shipped **pre-programmed** by Ruffus; there's nothing for you to configure on the card itself.

## Ordering cards

The same panel handles both kinds of request, and only an **owner** can make one — it can spend money.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cards you're still owed" icon="gift">
    If your store has fewer than its included 2 cards, ask for the rest. **No charge.** The request goes straight into the fulfilment queue.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Replacing a lost card" icon="rotate">
    Name one of your own active cards and we'll send a replacement. **\$20 CAD**, charged up front to your card on file, because the original was loaned and hasn't come back.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  A **revoked** card still counts against what your store has been loaned. Revoking a card you lost doesn't earn you a free one — that's what the replacement request is for.
</Note>

Two practical limits worth knowing before you click:

- **One open batch at a time.** While a request is still being encoded or is in transit, the panel won't offer another. Once it's delivered, you can request again.
- **A replacement needs a card on file.** It's the same card used for the NFC deposit; without one, the request is declined rather than queued.

Encoding never moves — a card you request is programmed by Ruffus and arrives ready to tap, exactly like the first two.

## Returning cards

Your NFC cards remain Ruffus property — they're loaned with your plan, not sold. There are two moments they come back to us:

- **You cancel your account** — all of your cards return.
- **You drop a location** — moving to a plan with fewer locations returns only _that_ location's cards. Cards at the locations you keep stay exactly where they are. On a paid plan the drop takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and that's when we ask for the cards; during your free trial the change is immediate.

Either way, the process is the same:

<Steps>
  <Step title="We email you">
    You get an email naming which cards are involved, with a link to request a **free prepaid Canada Post label**. Sending them back costs you nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You have 30 days">
    Reminders go out at 7, 21, and 28 days if we haven't heard from you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unreturned cards are billed">
    After the 30 days, cards that haven't come back are charged at **\$20 CAD each** to your card on file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A dropped location's cards stop working the moment that location is deactivated — a tap there fails immediately, returned or not. The return window is only about getting the physical cards back.
</Note>

Two situations that look like they'd trigger a return but don't:

- **A seasonal pause** — you keep every card. Pausing isn't cancelling, and no return window opens.
- **Your free trial ending without a plan chosen** — we never auto-charge you for a subscription. Your program pauses instead, and you keep your cards. The only charges that ever reach your card on file are the per-card fees above.

## Compatibility

Most recent phones support tap-to-read out of the box:

- **Android** — any NFC-capable phone with Chrome.
- **iPhone XS and newer** — works with the screen on (the phone can be locked). Older iPhones (7/8 and similar) can't read NFC in the background — those customers use the printed QR poster instead, which always stays active as a fallback.

## What if...

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A customer taps twice in a row?">
    The second tap is treated like any repeat scan — if you have a cooldown set, they'll see a friendly "already stamped" screen rather than earning twice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Someone reuses an old tap (screenshot, browser history)?">
    Each tap's signature is single-use. Reopening a previous tap shows a "tap again" screen instead of granting a stamp.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which of my loyalty cards does a tap stamp?">
    The one that station card is assigned to. If you run more than one card, each NFC card is tied to a specific one when it's programmed, so a tap never has to guess and never lands on the wrong card. The same is true of every other entry point — a poster QR code and a printable stamp code are each tied to one card too. If you run a single card, there's nothing to choose and everything points at it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a tap trigger referral bonuses or points/tier bonuses?">
    Not yet — v1 NFC taps grant the base stamp only. Referral and points/tier bonuses aren't available during the initial pilot; once they are, they'll apply the same way to QR-scanned and staff-approved stamps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a customer redeem a reward by tapping?">
    Yes — a tap is one of the two ways a customer proves they're at your counter to redeem (scanning the QR sticker on a till or tablet is the other). If their card is already full, a tap doesn't grant a stamp — a full card can't earn a new one until it's redeemed — it opens the redemption confirmation instead. The customer confirms, then a staff member holds the **Hold to validate** button on their screen to release the reward. See [Stamps and Rewards](/loyalty-card/stamps-and-rewards#how-redemption-works).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
