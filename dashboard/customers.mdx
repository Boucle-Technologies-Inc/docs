Every person who joins your loyalty card shows up in **Members** — searchable, sortable, and exportable, with a full history for each individual customer.

## The Members list

Each row shows:

- **Email** (masked for privacy — you'll see something like `j***@gmail.com`, not the full address)
- **Lifetime stamps** and **current stamps** (their progress toward the next reward)
- **Last seen** and **joined** dates
- **Rewards redeemed**
- **Engagement** — a High/Mid/Low badge derived from how recently they last visited (High: within 14 days, Mid: within 45 days, Low: beyond that)

Above the table, three stat chips — **Total**, **Active**, and **This month** — give you headline counts for the full list, independent of any filter or search applied below them.

You can search the list and sort by lifetime stamps, last seen, or joined date — handy for finding your most loyal customers or anyone who's gone quiet.

## Filtering into segments

Above the list, filter chips let you narrow the list down to a specific segment, each showing a live count:

- **All**
- **Close to reward** — 1–2 stamps remaining before their next reward
- **Reward unclaimed** — completed a card but haven't redeemed the reward yet
- **Inactive 7d\+** — no visit in the last 7 days
- **Inactive 30d\+** — no visit in the last 30 days

Segments combine with search, so you can filter to a segment and then search within it to find a specific customer.

<Tip>
  The **At-risk** and **Close to reward** cards on the Dashboard overview link straight into the matching segment here — a fast way to go from a summary number to the actual list of customers behind it. The At-risk card also has its own bulk **send reminder** button, letting you manually trigger the same inactivity-nudge email the automated weekly job sends, for the members currently listed there.
</Tip>

## Member detail timeline

Click into any member to see their recent activity: their **last 20 stamps** and **last 10 redemptions**, in order. It's the fastest way to answer "has this customer actually been coming in?" during a support conversation — though for a customer with a longer history, the timeline shows their most recent activity, not the complete record.

## Exporting your member list

You can export your Members list as a CSV, up to 5,000 rows at a time, via the **Export CSV** button — columns are email, lifetime stamps, current stamps, last visit, redeemed, and member-since date. If you need activity over a specific date range instead — stamps, approvals, redemptions — the **Activity** page has its own CSV export with date-range filtering.

<Tip>
  Keep an export handy. If your subscription ever lapses, the cancellation and grace-period-warning emails Ruffus sends you include a separate export link (owner-only) for pulling your customer list before data is purged — see [Plans & Billing](/billing/plans). That export's column set is different from the in-dashboard button above: email, first name, locale, marketing consent, joined date, current stamps, lifetime stamps, and card.
</Tip>

## Win-back emails (automatic)

Ruffus automatically brings inactive customers back for you — there's nothing to configure to turn this on:

- If a customer hasn't visited in **30\+ days**, they get an automatic win-back email and push notification.
- A customer won't be re-nudged more than once every 60 days, so nobody gets spammed.
- All marketing messages are consent-gated with an unsubscribe link, in line with Québec's CASL rules.

The one thing you _can_ customize is a short personal note — up to 280 characters — that gets appended to the win-back email. You'll find it under **Dashboard → Campaigns → Win-back**, alongside an audience preview and send history.

<Note>
  There's no threshold selector (you can't change the 30-day window) and no template editor. The win-back email is designed and sent automatically — your customization is limited to that personal note.
</Note>

Ruffus also sends two other automatic emails, unrelated to inactivity: a "your reward is ready" email the moment a card is completed, and a reminder if an earned reward sits unclaimed for 14\+ days.

## Campaigns

For promos beyond win-back, head to **Dashboard → Campaigns**. You can run bonus-stamp or new-member-bonus promotions, scoped to a specific location and a start/end date. See [Multi-Location](/dashboard/multi-location) if you're running campaigns across more than one location, and [Campaigns & Automated Messages](/dashboard/campaigns) for the full picture — including every automatic lifecycle email and push Ruffus sends beyond win-back.

## Staff access to members

The Members page isn't in a manager's sidebar — it's an owner-only nav item, so managers don't see a link into it day to day. That said, the underlying member-detail and send-reminder endpoints only require a staff login, not specifically an owner, so a manager who has a direct link to a member isn't blocked by the API itself. In practice, member management is an owner tool; managers work the counter. Staff working the counter don't have dashboard logins at all — they use 6-digit PINs created at **Dashboard → Staff**, which only gate stamp approvals and redemptions, not access to your customer data. There's no email-invite flow for staff; PINs are the whole model.

## Privacy

Member data is private to your account. Ruffus operates under Québec's Law 25, and customers can request erasure of their own record at any time — it's self-serve from their account, no need to email you.
